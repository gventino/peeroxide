{title}
{underline}

Share your screen (or a single window), and optionally its sound, with
friends on the same network, including virtual LANs such as Radmin VPN or
Hamachi.

Requirements: Windows 10 version 2004 or newer, or Windows 11. No installation.
Build: {commit}


NEW: SOUND
----------
- You can now share the sound of what you broadcast (see SHARING YOUR
  SCREEN). Viewers get a volume slider and a mute button.
- This version can't see or connect to 0.3.0 or older, so everyone you
  share with needs this version too. Mixing versions shows "incompatible
  version".
- Your ID, name, settings and saved people carry over.
- If you unzip to a new folder, Windows Firewall asks again: tick both
  boxes again (see FIRST RUN).
- Close the old version before opening this one.


UPDATES
-------
- Every time you open Peeroxide it checks for a new version. If there is
  one, it downloads it, installs it and restarts by itself; you only see a
  progress bar for a few seconds. Click "Skip" to open right away instead.
- Only updates signed by the Peeroxide publisher are installed.
- No internet? The app opens normally after a few seconds.


FIRST RUN
---------
1. Unzip anywhere and double-click peeroxide.exe.

2. "Windows protected your PC": the app isn't code-signed.
   Click "More info" -> "Run anyway".

   To avoid that warning: right-click peeroxide.exe (or the zip, before
   unzipping) -> Properties -> tick "Unblock" at the bottom -> OK.
   Only do this for files you downloaded from the official Peeroxide
   release page. You only need it once: updates installed by the app
   itself never show the warning.

3. Windows Firewall prompt: tick BOTH boxes, "Private networks" AND
   "Public networks", then click Allow.
   VPN adapters such as Radmin are usually classified as Public; if Public
   is not ticked, nobody can connect to you or see you in the list.

   Already clicked without ticking Public? Open Windows Security ->
   Firewall & network protection -> Allow an app through firewall ->
   Change settings -> find peeroxide -> tick Public -> OK.


SHARING YOUR SCREEN
-------------------
- Under "Broadcast", pick a Source (a monitor or a window) and a Quality,
  then click "Start broadcasting".
- Over Radmin VPN / Hamachi, choose "Internet / VPN - 720p - 20 fps":
  about 2 Mbps of upload, enough for most home connections.
- To share sound, tick "Share audio" BEFORE you start. It is off by
  default and remembered. What it shares depends on the source:
    * a window:  only the sound of that window's app;
    * a monitor: all sound on your PC (except Peeroxide itself), which
                 includes voice calls and notifications;
    * your microphone is never shared.
  To turn sound on or off, stop broadcasting and start again.
- Nothing is captured or sent until someone starts watching.


WATCHING
--------
- People who are broadcasting appear under "Broadcasting on this network".
  Click a name to watch; click another name to switch.
- If they share sound, a speaker button (mute) and a volume slider appear
  under "Watching ...". They only change what you hear, and are remembered.
  Otherwise it says "The broadcaster isn't sharing audio".
- Fullscreen: press F11, double-click the picture, or click "Fullscreen".
  Move the mouse to show the volume and an exit button; Esc leaves.
- Next to each name there is an ID like 7268-E22A. The broadcaster sees
  their own ID at the top of their window. If they match, you are watching
  the right person (the app refuses to connect if the ID is faked).


SAVED
-----
- Everyone you watch is saved automatically (shown with a star).
- If a saved person is broadcasting but doesn't show up in the list, look
  under "Saved" and click their name to connect. The trash icon forgets them.
- A red warning icon next to a name means someone is using the name of a
  person you saved, but with a different ID. That happens after a
  reinstall, but it could also be an impersonation: confirm the ID with them.


IF SOMEONE NEVER SHOWS UP
-------------------------
Connect manually once; they are saved from then on.
1. The broadcaster clicks "Copy connect string" and picks the entry for the
   network you share - for Radmin, the one named "Radmin VPN" (26.x.x.x).
2. They send you that text (chat, Discord, ...).
3. You open "Connect manually", paste it, and click Watch.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Anyone who can reach you on the network can watch, and hear, while you
  broadcast. Stop broadcasting when you're done.
- Settings, your ID, saved people and logs are stored in:
  %APPDATA%\Peeroxide\data
