{underline}

Share your screen (or a single window) with friends on the same network,
including virtual LANs such as Radmin VPN or Hamachi.

Requirements: 64-bit Linux with X11 or Wayland, PipeWire and
xdg-desktop-portal (screen capture), ALSA or PipeWire (sound). No
installation. Linux builds are new and barely tested: please report what
doesn't work.
Build: {commit}


NEW IN 0.6.1
------------
- The first Linux package. It updates itself from now on.


FIRST RUN
---------
1. Unzip anywhere you can write to (e.g. ~/Apps/peeroxide) and run
   ./peeroxide from that folder.
2. If you use a firewall (ufw, firewalld), allow Peeroxide on your LAN or
   VPN interface, or nobody can connect to you or see you in the list.


UPDATES
-------
- Every time you open Peeroxide it checks for a new version. If there is
  one, it downloads it, installs it in the same folder and restarts by
  itself. Click "Skip" to open right away instead.
- Only updates signed by the Peeroxide publisher are installed.
- In a folder you can't write to (e.g. /opt), it offers the download page
  instead. Start with --no-update to turn the check off.


SHARING YOUR SCREEN
-------------------
- Under "Broadcast", pick a Quality and click "Start broadcasting". On
  Wayland the system's screen-share dialog asks which monitor or window.
- Over Radmin VPN / Hamachi, choose "Internet / VPN - 720p - 24 fps".
- Broadcasts use H.264 on the processor; 1080p at 60 fps may not keep up.
- Sharing sound is only possible from Windows for now. You can hear
  broadcasts that share sound.
- Nothing is captured or sent until someone starts watching.


WATCHING
--------
- People who are broadcasting appear under "Broadcasting on this network".
  Click a name to watch; click another name to switch.
- Fullscreen: press F11, double-click the picture, or click "Fullscreen".
  Esc leaves.
- Next to each name there is an ID like 7268-E22A. The broadcaster sees
  their own ID at the top of their window. If they match, you are watching
  the right person (the app refuses to connect if the ID is faked).


IF SOMEONE NEVER SHOWS UP
-------------------------
Connect manually once; they are saved from then on.
1. The broadcaster clicks "Copy connect string" and picks the entry for the
   network you share.
2. They send you that text; you open "Connect manually", paste it, and
   click Watch.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Anyone who can reach you on the network can watch while you broadcast.
  Stop broadcasting when you're done.
- Settings, your ID, saved people and logs are stored in:
  ~/.local/share/peeroxide
