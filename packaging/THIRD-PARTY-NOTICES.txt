libde265 (H.265 video decoder)
------------------------------

peeroxide.exe contains libde265 1.1.3 (https://github.com/strukturag/libde265),
copyright struktur AG and the libde265 authors. It decodes the H.265 video
of the broadcasts you watch.

libde265 is free software, distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public
License, version 3 or (at your option) any later version. Its text follows,
together with the GNU General Public License version 3 it builds on.

libde265's source code, exactly as built into this program, is in
crates/de265-sys/vendor in Peeroxide's source code for this version (the
tag of the release). To use a modified libde265, change those files and
build Peeroxide from source as its README describes.
